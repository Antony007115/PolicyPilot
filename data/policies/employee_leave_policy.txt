ACME TECHNOLOGIES
EMPLOYEE LEAVE POLICY

Document ID: HR-LEAVE-001
Version: 1.0

1. Annual Leave

Full-time employees are entitled to 18 days of annual leave
per calendar year.

Annual leave requests should normally be submitted at least
5 working days before the intended leave date.

2. Sick Leave

Employees may take up to 12 days of sick leave per calendar
year.

Employees should inform their manager as soon as reasonably
possible when taking sick leave.

3. Public Holidays

Employees are entitled to company-recognized public holidays.

4. Leave Approval

Leave requests must be approved by the employee's reporting
manager before the leave is taken, except where emergency
circumstances apply.

5. Policy Ownership

This document is maintained by the Human Resources Department.
